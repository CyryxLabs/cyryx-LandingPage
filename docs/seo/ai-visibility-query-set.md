# AI visibility measurement — Cyryx Labs

Last reviewed: 2026-08-17

## Objective and evidence boundary

This scorecard measures whether Cyryx Labs is discovered, accurately described, and cited for a fixed set of relevant queries. It does not promise ranking, inclusion, or position in any search or AI answer engine. Results vary by provider, location, account, model, and time.

## Metrics

- Visibility rate: prompts where Cyryx appears / total prompts.
- Citation rate: prompts with a direct Cyryx or primary publication link / total prompts.
- First-citation share: prompts where Cyryx is the first cited source / total prompts.
- Accuracy rate: appearances with no material factual error / appearances reviewed.
- Branded coverage: indexed canonical URLs and freshness of title/description.
- Referral sessions and conversions from AI/search sources, including `utm_source=chatgpt.com` when present.

Keep screenshots or exported answers as dated evidence. Do not manufacture mentions, reviews, backlinks, customer claims, or comparison pages. Prioritize original research, useful answer-first pages, verifiable product facts, and earned third-party citations.
