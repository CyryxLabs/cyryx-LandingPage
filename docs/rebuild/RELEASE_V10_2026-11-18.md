## Scope of the release

- Home v10: hero with a still metallic plate, "what we do" scene, two paths,
  scroll-walked workflow demo (sample data), solutions, products (Onyx, AEXOS
  Core, Orbit as name plus "In development"), evidence, delivery, company, close.

  (at least 901 x 700) and on phones and tablets at least 640 px tall.
- Static page, with every panel visible, for: reduced motion, Save-Data,
  low-performance devices, shorter screens, and no JavaScript.
- Signature footer, new navigation, /demos, process-language claims (v9 work).
