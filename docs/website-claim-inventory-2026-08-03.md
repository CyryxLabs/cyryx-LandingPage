# Cyryx Labs Website Claim Inventory

**Review date:** 2026-08-03
**Scope:** Material public claims introduced or materially reframed by the website-excellence branch, including visible copy, structured data, research records, legal copy, and `llms.txt`.
**Rule:** A visual concept, publication abstract, route name, or internal positioning decision is not proof of implementation, adoption, performance, certification, or client outcome.

## Evidence states

- **Verified** — directly supported by a named, reviewable primary artifact for the precise claim.
- **Qualified** — supported only for a bounded purpose, maturity state, repository snapshot, or internal positioning decision; the public wording must retain that boundary.
- **Withheld** — not supported well enough for publication as a factual or outcome claim.

## Release rule

Before publication, the release owner must confirm that every changed material claim maps to this register, that no **withheld** claim appears as factual copy, and that every **qualified** claim retains its stated boundary. A production smoke test is required before changing any code-level flow from **qualified** to **verified in production**.
